Deep LearningEquitiesPyTorch

Mixed-Frequency Deep Learning for Cross-Sectional Stock Prediction

A fusion architecture (MLP for quarterly financials + CNN-LSTM for daily price/volume, combined by self-attention) trained with a pairwise ranking objective on CSI 300 constituents.

  • +26% Mean IC and +33% RankIC from adding fundamentals to a price-only baseline (627k observations)
  • Backtest under simplified assumptions: 144.7% annualized, Sharpe 2.98 vs. 26.2% / 1.62 for the equal-weight benchmark
  • Full ablation, ranking-loss design and honest backtest caveats in the report

深度学习股票PyTorch

面向横截面股票预测的混频深度学习模型

将季度财务报表（MLP 分支）与日频量价数据（CNN-LSTM 分支）通过自注意力机制融合， 并以成对排序损失训练，用于沪深 300 成分股的横截面排序预测。

  • 相比纯量价基线，引入财务信息使 Mean IC 提升约 26%、RankIC 提升约 33%（62.7 万个观测）
  • 简化假设下回测年化 144.7%，夏普 2.98；等权基准为 26.2% / 1.62
  • 报告包含完整消融实验、排序损失设计与诚实的回测局限说明

CryptoOn-Chain DataMarket Microstructure

On-Chain MEV Signals for Cryptocurrency Timing

Do Ethereum sandwich attacks predict BTC volatility? 3 MEV-native features plus 26 technical factors, de-correlated with Jaccard-similarity spectral clustering, backtested with cost sensitivity analysis.

  • MEV features show |IC| 0.10–0.27 and near-orthogonality to classical price factors
  • Strongest channel is volatility timing, not direction — MEV measures market fragility
  • Break-even at ~0.23 bp of fees: a clean demonstration that alpha is execution-bound

加密资产链上数据市场微观结构

链上 MEV 信号与加密货币择时

以太坊三明治攻击能否预测 BTC 波动？3 个 MEV 原生特征加上 26 个技术因子， 以基于 Jaccard 相似的谱聚类去相关，并进行成本敏感性回测。

  • MEV 特征 |IC| 0.10–0.27，且与经典量价因子接近正交
  • 最稳健的通道是波动率择时而非方向——MEV 度量的是市场脆弱性
  • 盈亏平衡费率约 0.23 bp：alpha 真实存在，但受限于执行环境
